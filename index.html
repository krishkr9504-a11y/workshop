Before you order

How do I pay?

Cash on Delivery only, right now. You pay the delivery person when your order arrives — nothing upfront.

How will I know my order is confirmed?

After you fill the order form, WhatsApp opens with your order details pre-filled. Send it, and we confirm within a few hours.

What are the sizes available?

Most pieces come in S–XL. Exact sizing is listed on each product before you order.

Where all do you deliver?

We deliver across all of Bihar — Patna, Gaya, Muzaffarpur, Bhagalpur, and everywhere in between. Delivery usually takes 3–6 working days depending on your city.

Get in touch

Questions about an order, sizing, or anything else — reach us directly. We usually reply within a few hours.
